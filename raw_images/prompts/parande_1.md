# Parandë 1 – image prompts

Paste-ready prompts for the pictures of chapter 1. The style, the setting and
the five characters are defined once in the [picture guide](README.md); each
prompt below is those blocks plus the scene, so the cast looks the same in
every picture. Follow the guide's steps to add a picture to the book.

The alt texts below are the ones in `_quarto-en.yml` and `_quarto-pt.yml`;
if a generated picture differs from the prompt, update both.

they show strangers in a generic town, and the cover still says *Quetelyë*.
They stay in the book until their new versions exist.

## 1. `parande_1_essi.jpeg` – *Mana esselya?*

**Scene.** The first lesson. A bright community-centre classroom with large
windows onto a seaside town; a whiteboard with nothing written on it. Rúmil
stands beside the board, one hand on his chest, introducing himself with a
smile. Four adult learners sit in a half circle on light wooden chairs:
Elenwë, Nerdanel, Voronwë and Elendil, each with a blank name tag on their
chest. Elenwë is half-raised, hand on her chest, answering. Empty speech
bubbles above Rúmil and Elenwë.

**Prompt.** `Warm watercolour and ink illustration, soft pastel palette,
clean brown outlines, friendly cartoon realism on textured paper. A bright
modern community-centre classroom with big windows showing a sunny seaside
town; an empty whiteboard. An older teacher with long silver hair tied back,
round glasses and a cardigan stands by the board with one hand on his chest,
smiling as he introduces himself; an empty speech bubble above him. Four
adult students of different ages and skin tones sit in a half circle on light
wooden chairs, each wearing a blank name tag: a young woman with golden hair
and a yellow jacket, half standing with her hand on her chest and an empty
speech bubble; a woman with red curly hair in green overalls; a man with dark
hair in a blue sailor's coat; a tall bearded man in a traveller's coat with a
suitcase beside his chair. All have slightly pointed ears except the bearded
man. No text, no letters, no writing anywhere. 16:9.`

**Alt text (EN).** A teacher with long silver hair and round glasses stands by
an empty whiteboard, hand on his chest, introducing himself to four adult
students sitting in a curved row; a young woman with golden hair answers him
with her hand on her chest. The students wear blank name tags.

**Alt text (PT).** Um professor de cabelo comprido prateado e óculos redondos
está junto a um quadro branco vazio, com a mão no peito, se apresentando a
quatro alunos adultos sentados em uma fileira curva; uma jovem de cabelo
dourado responde a ele com a mão no peito. Os alunos usam crachás em branco.

## 2. `parande_1_mallo.jpeg` – *Mallo tulilyë?*

**Scene.** Arrivals at the harbour of Avallónë on a bright morning. A quay
with white houses and a small lighthouse. A modern ferry is moored on the
right; a small white boat with a swan-shaped prow on the left. Travellers
come down the gangway with suitcases and backpacks: Elendil (bearded, coat,
suitcase) is greeted by Rúmil, who waves. Elenwë with a yellow backpack
checks a paper map that shows only coastlines and dots, no names. Gulls,
rope bollards, a café awning.

**Prompt.** `Warm watercolour and ink illustration, soft pastel palette,
clean brown outlines, friendly cartoon realism on textured paper. A sunny
morning on the quay of a small seaside town with white houses and a little
lighthouse. On the right a modern passenger ferry is moored; on the left a
small white boat with a swan-shaped prow. Travellers walk down a gangway with
suitcases and backpacks. A tall bearded man in a traveller's coat with a
suitcase is greeted by an older man with long silver hair and round glasses
who waves to him. A young woman with golden hair and a yellow backpack looks
at a paper map that shows only blank coastlines and dots. Gulls, rope
bollards, a café awning. No text, no letters, no signs, no writing anywhere.
16:9.`

**Alt text (EN).** Travellers with suitcases come down a ferry gangway onto a
sunny quay with white houses and a small lighthouse; a bearded man is greeted
by an older man with silver hair who waves, and a young woman with a yellow
backpack reads a paper map. A small boat with a swan-shaped prow is moored
nearby.

**Drift from the cast sheet.** Here Elendil wears a laced tunic and a sash,
and Rúmil has a ponytail and a browner cardigan with a waistcoat. Later
pictures follow the [picture guide](README.md).

**Alt text (PT).** Viajantes com malas descem a rampa de uma balsa para um
cais ensolarado com casas brancas e um pequeno farol; um homem de barba é
recebido por um homem mais velho de cabelo prateado que acena para ele, e uma
jovem com mochila amarela consulta um mapa de papel. Um pequeno barco com proa
em forma de cisne está atracado ao lado.

## 3. `parande_1_noti.jpeg` – *Nóti*

**Scene.** A pedestrian street at dusk with exactly **ten** round paper
lanterns strung in one straight line across the street, evenly spaced, each a
different soft colour, every one fully visible and clearly countable. Below,
Nerdanel and Voronwë sit at a café table and point up at the lanterns,
counting on their fingers. Keep the lanterns large and the background simple
so the count is unmistakable. (Check the count before using the picture;
regenerate if it is not ten.)

**Prompt.** `Warm watercolour and ink illustration, soft pastel palette,
clean brown outlines, friendly cartoon realism on textured paper. A quiet
pedestrian street at dusk. Exactly ten round paper lanterns hang in one
straight evenly spaced line across the street, each a different soft colour,
all fully visible, none overlapping, large and easy to count. Below them a woman 
with red curly hair in green overalls; a man with dark hair in a blue sailor's coat. 
All have slightly pointed ears  sit at a small café table and point up at the lanterns,
counting on their fingers. Simple background, no other lights. No text, no
letters, no signs, no writing anywhere. 16:9.`

**Drift from the cast sheet.** Voronwë's coat has no gold trim and his hair is
longer; Nerdanel wears an ochre top. Later pictures follow the
[picture guide](README.md).

**Alt text (EN).** Ten round paper lanterns in different colours hang in a row
across a street at dusk; below them a woman and a man at a café table point up
at the lanterns.

**Alt text (PT).** Dez lanternas de papel redondas de cores diferentes pendem
em fileira sobre uma rua ao anoitecer; por baixo, uma mulher e um homem em uma
mesa de café apontam para as lanternas.

## 4. `greetings.jpeg` – *Aiya!... ar Namárië!* (to redo)

**Scene.** Four panels on one sunny morning in Avallónë. The columns carry the
register and the rows carry the moment, so the picture reads like the table
in the chapter. Left column, friends (*Alla!* … *Á na márië!*, dialogue 1.2):
Elenwë and Nerdanel say hello, then goodbye. Right column, formal (*Aiya!* …
*Namárië!*, dialogue 1.3): Rúmil and Elendil say hello, then goodbye. Friends
wave and hug; the formal pair shake hands with a slight bow. No hand on the
chest: that gesture belongs to *Essenya ná…* in `parande_1_essi`.

**Prompt.**
```text
Warm ink-and-watercolour illustration on lightly textured cream paper; thin
warm-brown outlines; flat, soft pastel washes; airy composition with open
cream space; friendly cartoon realism. Present day: contemporary clothes,
streets and furniture.

A four-panel picture in a 2 x 2 grid with thin warm-brown panel borders and
narrow cream gutters, all on one sunny morning.

Setting: Avallónë, a small present-day harbour town: white clapboard and
plaster houses with grey and terracotta roofs, a stone quay with rope
bollards, a café with a pink-and-white striped awning, a slim white lighthouse
with a red top on a rocky headland, a pale stone tower on the green hill above
the town, calm blue sea, gulls.

Elenwë, a slim young elf woman in her early twenties, fair peach skin, rosy
cheeks, oval face, large dark-brown eyes, thin light-brown brows, small nose,
warm smile; long straight golden-blonde hair worn loose past the shoulders;
long slender pointed elf ears poking out sideways through the hair; open
mustard-yellow hip-length jacket with banded cuffs and hem over a white
T-shirt; light-blue cuffed jeans; mustard-yellow low-top canvas sneakers with
white toe caps.

Nerdanel, an elf woman in her early thirties, soft medium build, fair peachy
skin, rosy cheeks, soft round face, dark-brown eyes, thin arched auburn brows,
small upturned nose; voluminous bright copper-orange curly hair worn loose
past the shoulders; long sharply pointed elf ears angled up and back through
the curls; sage-green dungarees with a bib pocket, brass buckles and rolled
cuffs over a pale oatmeal-grey short-sleeved crew-neck T-shirt; coral-red
canvas high-top sneakers with white toe caps.

Rúmil, an elderly elf man who looks about seventy, tall and slim, fair warm
skin, lined clean-shaven face, warm smile, round thin dark-brown wire glasses;
long silver-white hair swept back, the top part in a small bun at the back of
the head, the rest loose past the shoulders; long leaf-shaped pointed elf ears
angled up and back, tips showing past the hair; open taupe-grey knit cardigan
with ribbed cuffs over a cream collared shirt, brown leather belt, tan
trousers, brown leather lace-up shoes.

Elendil, a tall broad-shouldered human man in his late forties with round
human ears, not pointed; light warm skin, long face, straight nose, thick dark
brows, no glasses; wavy shoulder-length grey-brown hair swept back with silver
streaks; full grey-brown beard with a silver streak down the chin; open
below-knee camel-tan coat with wide lapels over a sage-green crew-neck
T-shirt; charcoal-grey trousers tucked into tall brown cuffed boots.

Top left: Elenwë and Nerdanel meet on the seafront promenade, grinning; Elenwë
waves high with an open hand and Nerdanel opens her arms for a hug; an empty
speech bubble above each. Bottom left: the same two walk apart in opposite
directions, looking back over their shoulders with a quick casual wave; empty
speech bubbles. Top right: at the glass door of the community centre Rúmil and
Elendil stand upright facing each other and shake hands, each inclining his
head in a slight, respectful bow; Elendil holds a vintage tan leather
suitcase; empty speech bubbles. Bottom right: Rúmil, still at the door, gives
a small bow and raises one hand in farewell; Elendil, suitcase in hand, bows
slightly as he turns towards the harbour; empty speech bubbles.

No text, letters, numbers or writing anywhere: no signs, no labels, no
readable pages, no clock numerals. Speech bubbles, if any, are empty.

Landscape 16:9.
```
Negative prompt: the one in the [picture guide](README.md), plus `office,
business suit, children, black panel borders`. If the generator mixes up the
panels, generate each panel on its own with the same blocks and put the four
together in an image editor.

**Alt text (EN), for the new picture.** Four panels on a sunny morning in a
harbour town. On the left, two young women greet each other as friends, one
waving and one opening her arms for a hug, and later wave goodbye over their
shoulders. On the right, an older teacher with silver hair and a bearded man
with a suitcase shake hands with a slight bow at a glass door, and later part
with a small bow. The speech bubbles are empty.

**Alt text (PT), for the new picture.** Quatro painéis em uma manhã de sol em
uma cidade portuária. À esquerda, duas jovens se cumprimentam como amigas, uma
acenando e a outra abrindo os braços para um abraço, e depois se despedem
acenando por cima do ombro. À direita, um professor idoso de cabelo prateado e
um homem de barba com uma mala se cumprimentam com um aperto de mão e uma leve
reverência em uma porta de vidro, e depois se despedem com uma pequena
reverência. Os balões de fala estão vazios.

## 5. `daytime.jpeg` – *Lúmë* (to redo)

**Scene.** One day in Avallónë in seven panels, one for each time of day in
the chapter's table, in its order. The people in panels 5.1, 5.6 and 5.7 are
the speakers of dialogue 1.5. Time shows only through the light, the sky and
the sun or moon: no clocks. Seven panels with the same cast are too many for
one generation, so generate each panel on its own, landscape 4:3, and put
them together in an image editor:

- a portrait page like the current picture, two panels per row in the order
  5.1 to 5.6, and 5.7 across the full width at the bottom;
- above each panel, a plain cream label band in a thin brown frame, with the
  word in Tengwar and in Latin letters, as in the current picture;
- save the labelled page as `raw_images/daytime.jpeg` and the unlabelled
  panels in `raw_images/unlabelled/`.
