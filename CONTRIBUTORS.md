# Contributors

Everyone who has helped write *Ma Quetilyë Quenya?*, in any role: reviewers,
illustrators and voices included. One line per person, in the order people
joined.

Add your own line in your first pull request, with the name you want to be
credited by. The coordinator adds reviewers, and anyone whose work a maintainer
submitted for them. Your name also goes in the `SPDX-FileCopyrightText` line or
`.license` sidecar of the files you create.

Roles: coordinator, author, Quenya review, pedagogy review, translation,
illustration, voice, tech.

This table is also the credits page of every edition of the book, which
groups the people by role (`filters/credits.lua` builds it). Keep one person
per row and write the roles exactly as in the list above, separated by commas;
the Test check fails on any other role. A new role goes in the list above
and, at the same position, in `credits-roles` in every `_quarto-<lang>.yml`,
translated.

| Name | Roles | Chapters or editions |

## Tools

The project asks every contributor to say which generative tools they used, so
the same applies to the work done before the community joined: the chapter 1
text, the edition texts written with it (the reading guide in each
introduction, the tables of contents and the alt text), the first drafts of
the contributor documents and the picture prompts were drafted with an AI
assistant (Claude) and reviewed by the coordinator; the cover and the chapter 1
pictures were generated with Gemini from those prompts and retouched by hand.
None of this has had an independent review yet, and the cover and the pictures
are placeholders until they pass the reviews described in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). The credits page of each edition
(`docs/<lang>/credits.qmd`) says the same for the book's readers; change it
there too.
