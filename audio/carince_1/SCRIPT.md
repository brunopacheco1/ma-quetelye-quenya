# Carincë 1 – recording script

Ten tracks. The scene for every dialogue is Avallónë, today: a seaside town
where Quenya is the everyday language. Voices: **Rúmil** (older man, warm,
unhurried: the teacher), **Elenwë** (woman, bright), **Nerdanel** (woman,
calm), **Voronwë** (man, friendly), **Elendil** (man, polite, a visitor).

Stress falls on the second-to-last syllable in words of two syllables and on
the third-to-last in longer words unless the second-to-last is long (*Namárië*
→ na-MÁ-ri-ë, *Alqualondë* → al-qua-LON-dë, *Avallónë* → a-val-LÓ-në).

## 1.1 – `1-1.mp3` – greetings (word list)

One speaker, each word twice: normal, then slow.

> Alla! … Mesta! … Aiya! … Namárië!

## 1.2 – `1-2.mp3` – Elenwë and Nerdanel (informal)

Two friends meet in the street.

> **Elenwë:** Alla, Nerdanel!
> **Nerdanel:** Alla, Elenwë! Manen nalyë?
> **Elenwë:** Mára, hantanyel. Ar elyë?
> **Nerdanel:** Mára.
> **Elenwë:** Mesta, Nerdanel!
> **Nerdanel:** Mesta!

## 1.3 – `1-3.mp3` – Elendil and Rúmil (formal)

A visitor greets the teacher at the door of the school.

> **Elendil:** Aiya, Rúmil! Elen síla lúmenn' omentielvo.
> **Rúmil:** Aiya, Elendil! Elen síla lúmenn' omentielvo. Manen nalyë?
> **Elendil:** Mára, hantanyel. Ar elyë?
> **Rúmil:** Mára. Namárië, Elendil!
> **Elendil:** Namárië!

## 1.4 – `1-4.mp3` – times of day (word list)

One speaker. First the seven words, then the six greetings, each twice.

> amaurë … aurë … endaurë … apárilë … undómë … sinyë … lómë
>
> Mára amaurë! … Mára aurë! … Mára endaurë! … Mára apárilë! … Mára sinyë! … Mára lómë!

## 1.5 – `1-5.mp3` – three short exchanges

A pause of two seconds between exchanges.

> **Voronwë:** Mára amaurë, Nerdanel!
> **Nerdanel:** Mára amaurë, Voronwë!
>
> **Rúmil:** Mára sinyë, Elenwë!
> **Elenwë:** Mára sinyë, Rúmil! Manen nalyë?
> **Rúmil:** Mára, hantanyel.
>
> **Elendil:** Mára lómë, Voronwë!
> **Voronwë:** Mára lómë, Elendil! Namárië!

## 1.6 – `1-6.mp3` – first lesson: names

The teacher opens the class.

> **Rúmil:** Aiya! Essenya ná Rúmil. Nanyë istyar. Mana esselya?
> **Elenwë:** Aiya, Rúmil! Essenya ná Elenwë.
> **Rúmil:** Ar elyë? Mana esselya?
> **Voronwë:** Essenya ná Voronwë.
> **Rúmil:** Ar elyë?
> **Nerdanel:** Essenya ná Nerdanel.
> **Rúmil:** Ar elyë?
> **Elendil:** Essenya ná Elendil.
> **Rúmil:** Alla, Elenwë! Alla, Voronwë! Alla, Nerdanel! Alla, Elendil!

## 1.7 – `1-7.mp3` – where from

> **Rúmil:** Elenwë, mallo tulilyë?
> **Elenwë:** Tulin Valimarello. Nanyë Vanya.
> **Rúmil:** Ar elyë, Nerdanel? Mallo tulilyë?
> **Nerdanel:** Tulin Tirionello. Nanyë Noldo.
> **Rúmil:** Voronwë, mallo tulilyë?
> **Voronwë:** Tulin Endórello. Nanyë Noldo.
> **Rúmil:** Ma quetilyë Quenya?
> **Voronwë:** Ná, quetin Quenya ar Sindarin.
> **Rúmil:** Ar elyë, Elendil? Mallo tulilyë?
> **Elendil:** Tulin Númenórello. Nanyë Atan.
> **Rúmil:** Ma quetilyë Sindarin?
> **Elendil:** Ui, lá quetin Sindarin. Quetin Quenya.

## 1.8 – `1-8.mp3` – numbers (word list)

One speaker, one to ten, each twice.

> minë … atta … neldë … canta … lempë … enquë … otso … tolto … nertë … quëan

## 1.9 – `1-9.mp3` – sounds

One speaker. For each row of the *Lammar* table: the sound on its own, then
the example words, slowly.

> a: Aman, Atan · á: mára, málo · e: Elda, Elendil · é: nér · i: Tirion, minë · í: nís, síla · o: Noldo, otso · ó: lómë, Endórë · u: tulin, undómë · ú: Rúmil, Númenórë · ai: aiya · au: aurë, amaurë · oi: Oiolossë · ui: ui, Uinen · eu: leuca · c: canta, cenda · qu: Quenya, quëan · y: Vanya, aiya

## 1.10 – `1-10.mp3` – exercise 1 (listen and write)

Four greetings, numbered aloud, with a three-second pause after each.

> Minë. — Mára sinyë, Nerdanel!
> Atta. — Mára amaurë, Rúmil!
> Neldë. — Mára lómë, Elenwë!
> Canta. — Mára endaurë, Voronwë!
